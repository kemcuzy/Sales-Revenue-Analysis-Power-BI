Sales & Revenue Analysis | Power BI

Project Overview

This project analyses Karcool Group's 2014 U.S. sales data using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to examine sales performance and communicate business insights through a structured, multi-page report.


Business Questions

1. How does revenue vary across months and quarters?

2. Which product categories contribute the most revenue?

3. Which customers are the leading sales contributors?

4. How does performance differ across U.S. regions?

5. What does the payment-method distribution reveal?


Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop

- Power Query and DAX, where applied

- Interactive data visualization


Key Findings

- Reported total revenue exceeded $435,000.

- December recorded $66,642.78 in reported revenue.

- February recorded $19,985.50 in reported revenue.

- Beverages was the leading reported category at $119,577.11.

- Nancy Freehafer was the leading reported customer at $104,252.34.

- The West region generated approximately $91,250.


Business Recommendations

a. Investigate seasonal sales fluctuations.

b. Review product demand, cost, and margin together.

c. Monitor customer concentration and retention.

d. Investigate differences in regional performance.

e. Extend the report with profitability measures when cost data is available.



Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can help transform sales data into a structured analytical story that supports business investigation and decision-making.
